FAQ

How do I use Tashriq in GarageBand or Logic?
Create a new instrument track and choose the AUv3 plug-in “تشريق: Tashriq Instrument”. To play another instrument in maqams, use “تشريق: Tashriq MIDI FX” as a MIDI effect; for sympathetic resonance on any sound, use “تشريق: Tashriq Resonance” as an audio effect.

How do I connect a MIDI keyboard?
Connect it to the iPad's USB-C port, or over Bluetooth from the app's sound settings; it is detected automatically.

Does the app need the internet?
No, everything works offline.
